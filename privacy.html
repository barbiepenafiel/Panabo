Privacy Notice

How this prototype handles your information.

This prototype collects nothing

This is a student design prototype. The contact form does not send or store what you type. The only thing saved is your text-size and high-contrast choice, kept in your own browser so the setting stays when you change pages.

In the final website

A real city website would explain what personal data it collects through forms, why, how long it is kept, and how residents can exercise their rights under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), including who the City’s Data Protection Officer is. Placeholder section
