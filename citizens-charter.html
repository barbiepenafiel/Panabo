Citizen’s Charter

The Citizen’s Charter tells you, for each city service, what to bring, the steps, the fees and how long it takes. It is required under the Ease of Doing Business Act (Republic Act No. 11032).

The steps below show how the final page could present each service. Sample content See the official Citizen’s Charter on panabocity.gov.ph under the Government menu.

Business permit (new application)

Office: Business Permits and Licensing Office

  1. Fill out the Business Permit Application Form or apply on eBOSS.
  2. Submit requirements and have your assessment done.
  3. Pay the assessed fees at the City Treasurer’s Office.
  4. Receive your business permit.

Fees and processing time: to be filled in from the official charter.

Copy of birth, marriage or death certificate

Office: City Civil Registry Office

  1. Fill out the Civil Registry Document Request.
  2. Pay the fee.
  3. Claim the certified copy.
Community Tax Certificate (cedula)

Office: Office of the City Treasurer

  1. Fill out the Cedula Request.
  2. Pay the computed tax.
  3. Receive your cedula.
