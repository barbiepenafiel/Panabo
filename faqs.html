Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions. Sample answers for the prototype

How do I apply for a business permit?

Download the Business Permit Application Form, or start online through the e-Government Portal (eBOSS). Requirements are listed in the Citizen’s Charter.

Where can I get a copy of my birth certificate?

Request it from the City Civil Registry Office using the Civil Registry Document Request form.

Who do I call in an emergency?

See the Emergency Hotlines page for CDRRMO, police, fire and coast guard numbers.

How do I send feedback about a city service?

Use the Contact Us form. Choose “Feedback about a city service” as the topic.

Where are executive orders and budgets posted?

See Executive Orders and Public Documents under Transparency.
